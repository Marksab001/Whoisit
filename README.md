Whoisit helps you review your Instagram follower and following activity without sending your account details to a server.

- Compare the accounts that follow you with the accounts you follow.
- Find accounts you follow that do not follow you back.
- See accounts that follow you but that you do not follow back.
- Review mutual followers in a clear list.
- Check follower and following changes over time.
- Use a browser-based interface that is simple to open and use.
- Keep your Instagram password private.
- Avoid cookie uploads and account credential sharing.
- Use an open-source tool built with JavaScript and Manifest V3.

Whoisit is designed for everyday computer users. You do not need programming knowledge to download it, open it on Windows, and use it in your browser.

Whoisit is built around local use. It runs in your browser, which means your Instagram follower and following checks happen on your own computer. The application does not ask you to type your Instagram password into a separate form. It also does not ask you to upload cookies.

This privacy-first approach is useful if you want to check follower activity without handing over login details to a third-party website. Your browser handles the local work, and Whoisit focuses on showing you the follower and following information you want to review.
